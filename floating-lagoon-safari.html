The Experience

A slow boat through one of the East Coast's quietest wild places

Arugam Bay isn't only known for its waves. Two kilometres inland, the lagoon spreads into a maze of mangrove channels, sandbanks and open water that wakes up long before the surf does. We take small boats in, engines low, so the lagoon barely notices we're there.

Mornings belong to the wildlife — water monitors and crocodiles sunning on the banks, painted storks and brahminy kites working the shallows, the occasional troop of macaques moving through the mangrove roots. Evenings belong to the sky, as the lagoon turns into a mirror for the sunset over Pottuvil.

Every safari is led by guides who grew up on this water and know it by season, not by schedule.

Sunrise Safari

  • Best for photos & couples

Getting There

Free pickup from anywhere in Arugam Bay

Pickup zone

Free tuk-tuk pickup from hotels and guesthouses along Main Street, Whiskey Point, Pottuvil and Ulla.

Pickup timing

We collect guests 30 minutes before departure — 5:30 AM for sunrise, 3:00 PM for sunset safaris.

Launch point

Safaris depart from our jetty on Arugam Bay Lagoon, a short walk from the Pottuvil–Panama road.

Not staying nearby?

We also arrange pickup from Panama and surrounding areas — just ask when you message us.
